Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis 2022 (Excel Dashboard)
Objective

Vrinda Store wants an annual sales report for 2022 to understand its customers and grow sales in 2023.

Dataset
31,000+ order records (Jan-Dec 2022)
Columns: Order ID, Cust ID, Gender, Age, Age Group, Date, Month, Status, Channel, SKU, Category, Size, Qty, Amount, ship-city, ship-state and more

Key Insights
Total sales of about Rs. 2.1 Cr; women contribute ~64% of revenue
Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states (~37% of sales)
Adults (30-49 yrs) are the largest customer segment (~50% of orders)
Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart drive ~80% of sales
"Set" is the best-selling category (~50% of revenue)
~92% of orders are delivered; ~8% are cancelled, returned or refunded
Monthly sales peaked in March and declined towards December
Recommendation

Target women aged 30-49 in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh with ads and coupons on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.
