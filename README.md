## Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis 2022 (Excel Dashboard)

## Objective

Vrinda Store wants an annual sales report for 2022 to understand its customers and grow sales in 2023.

## Dataset
31,000+ order records (Jan-Dec 2022)
Columns: Order ID, Cust ID, Gender, Age, Age Group, Date, Month, Status, Channel, SKU, Category, Size, Qty, Amount, ship-city, ship-state and more

## Steps Involved
 1. Data Cleaning
 2. Data Processing
 3. Data Analysis
 4. Interactive Dashboard
 5. Insights & Reporting

## Questions Answered:-
 1. Compare sales and orders using a single chart
 2. Which month had the highest sales and orders?
 3. Who purchased more in 2022: men or women?
 4. What are the different order statuses in 2022?
 5. Top 10 states contributing to sales
 6. Relationship between age and gender based on number of orders
 7. Which channel contributes the most to sales?
 8. Highest selling category
    
## Key Insights

 1. Total sales of about Rs. 2.1 Cr; women contribute ~64% of revenue
 2. Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states (~37% of sales)
 3. Adults (30-49 yrs) are the largest customer segment (~50% of orders)
 4. Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart drive ~80% of sales
 5. "Set" is the best-selling category (~50% of revenue)
 6. ~92% of orders are delivered; ~8% are cancelled, returned or refunded
 7. Monthly sales peaked in March and declined towards December

## Recommendation

Target women aged 30-49 in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh with ads and coupons on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.
